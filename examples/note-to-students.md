# Note to students

The note the case-study course posts on Canvas next to the link to the course
assistant. It is adapted from the course's own page for the assistant
(originally in Norwegian; the English below keeps the same points). Replace
the course name, the tool name and the answer-key line, and post it where the
link lives.

---

## The course assistant

**What it is.** An AI chatbot set up for this course only. It has the course's
own instructions and course material (the schedule, the slides, the video
transcripts, the formula sheet and the exercise questions), and it has been
told to help you understand rather than to answer for you. It runs in Sikt
KI-assistent, the AI service NHH recommends. Log in with Feide.

**What it is good for.** Explaining a concept in more than one way, making
new practice problems, quizzing you one question at a time, finding the hole
in a piece of reasoning you have written, and answering practical questions
about the course from the course files.

**What it will not do.** It will not give you the solution to an exercise, a
case or an assignment. It will give you a hint and ask what you have tried.
That is on purpose: the exercises are where the learning happens.

**It can be wrong.** Language models can miscalculate and can state a wrong
claim with full confidence. Check every number. The answer key in this course
is the videos, the formula sheet and the textbook, not the assistant.

**Your chats are yours.** The course responsible cannot see what you write.
Your chat history is kept in your own account in Sikt KI-assistent. Do not paste personal
data about yourself or others into it.

**This is the course's sanctioned AI.** Using it is within the course's rules
for generative AI, which you find in the Canvas document "Generative AI
regulations – [course code]". The rules for individual assignments are in
that document too, and they apply whichever AI tool you use.

**Good first messages.** Try one of these, or write your own.

- "Explain the difference between the internal rate of return and the
  required rate of return as if I had never taken finance. Use a numerical
  example with one investment today and one payment in a year."
- "Quiz me on the effects of tax and debt. Ask five questions, one at a time,
  wait for my answer before the next, and correct me as we go."
- "Make three practice problems on declining-balance depreciation and the tax
  shield, at the level of an introductory finance course. Show the answers
  only when I ask."
- "I am stuck on NPV profiles: why can two projects swap ranking when the
  required return changes, if their IRRs are fixed? Explain with a figure
  described in words, then give me a check question."
